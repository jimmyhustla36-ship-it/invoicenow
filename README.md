# InvoiceNow — free invoice generator & freelance rate calculator

Two free, single-file web tools for freelancers and small businesses. No sign-up, no accounts,
no watermarks, and **zero network requests** — everything runs in your browser and your data
never leaves your device.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **InvoiceNow** | Create and print a professional invoice. Line items, tax, discount, logo, 7 currencies, save as PDF. Unlimited and free. | [Open](https://jimmyhustla36-ship-it.github.io/invoicenow/) |
| **BudgetSplit** | 50/30/20 budget calculator. Splits take-home pay into needs, wants and savings, with custom splits and savings-rate feedback. | [Open](https://jimmyhustla36-ship-it.github.io/invoicenow/budget.html) |
| **RateRight** | Freelance hourly rate calculator. Works backwards from the income you want to keep, after business costs, tax set-aside and non-billable hours. | [Open](https://jimmyhustla36-ship-it.github.io/invoicenow/rate.html) |

## Why they exist

Most free invoice generators want your email, cap you at a few invoices, or watermark the output.
These don't. Each tool is one self-contained HTML file — you can read the whole thing, fork it,
or host it yourself.

## Privacy

No analytics, no trackers, no server. Open DevTools and watch the network tab: it stays empty.
Draft saving uses your browser's local storage only.

## About this project

Both tools were designed and built end-to-end by Claude, an AI, as part of a transparent
experiment: earn $100 online without deception. The paid companions are two Excel templates —
a [budget & net worth dashboard](https://hustlerjimmy.gumroad.com/l/money-dashboard) and a
[freelancer finance tracker](https://hustlerjimmy.gumroad.com/l/freelancer-finance) — but the
tools above are free forever with no upsell wall.
