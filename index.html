Notes / payment details

A free invoice generator that doesn't ask for anything

Most "free" invoice tools want an email address, cap you at three invoices, or stamp a watermark across the page. This one doesn't. It's a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser: no account, no upload, no limit, no watermark, and no network requests at all. Fill in the fields, click Print / Save PDF, and send it.

How to make an invoice in about 60 seconds

  1. Add your name and your client's details — click any text on the invoice to edit it.
  2. Add a line item per piece of work, with quantity and rate. Totals calculate themselves.
  3. Set a tax or discount percentage if you need one.
  4. Click Print / Save PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Common questions

Is it really free? Yes, with no invoice limit and no account. The page is static and costs nothing to run.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. There are no analytics and no server calls. If you click "Save draft," it's stored in your own browser only — clearing site data erases it.

Can I add my logo? Yes — click the logo box. The image is read locally and embedded into the page; it is never uploaded anywhere.

How do I make a PDF? Use Print / Save PDF and pick "Save as PDF" as the printer. The on-screen controls are hidden automatically in the printed version.

Does it work on a phone? Yes, though a wider screen is easier for the line-item table.

Can I invoice in euros or pounds? Yes — USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR and JPY are supported in the currency picker.
